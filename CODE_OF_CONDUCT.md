# Code of conduct

Adu Labs aims to provide a focused, respectful environment for learning and
building reliable systems.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, licenses, and attribution.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, spam, and
  deliberate disruption.
- Never pressure contributors to reveal private identities or employment
  information.

## Scope

This policy applies to Adu Labs repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other spaces represented as part of the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate this
policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. For a private
conduct report, use the repository's private security-reporting channel and
mark the report as a code-of-conduct concern.
